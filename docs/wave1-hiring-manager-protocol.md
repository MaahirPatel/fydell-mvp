# Wave 1 hiring-manager protocol

Do not explain the product first. Sit with 3–5 hiring managers who hire analysts or similar IC roles.

## Task

1. Open the public site.
2. Identify what Fydell sells, in their own words.
3. Request a pilot or sign in, then invite a candidate to DA-01.
4. Open the resulting report and interpret it.
5. Explain why this is more useful than a résumé plus interview.
6. Say whether they would run a paid pilot.

## Watch, do not prompt

Record:

- Time to first correct product description
- Time to successful invite
- Whether they confuse Evaluations with a marketplace
- Whether they treat analysis as a hire/no-hire decision
- Where they stall
- Exact words on willingness to pay

Compliments are not evidence. Confusion, time-to-invite, report comprehension, and willingness to pilot are.

## Status

Not run. This requires real hiring managers. It is a Wave 1 closure gate, not a later-wave extra.
